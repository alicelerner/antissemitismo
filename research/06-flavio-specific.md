# Axis 6: Everything attributable to Flávio Bolsonaro himself (Jews, Israel, Holocaust, Nazism, dictatorship, religious minorities, the people he keeps around)

Research date: 2026-10-05. Author: research agent (Claude). Language: English; quotes in original plus translation.

This axis decides how much of the site can say "Flávio" versus "the project Flávio inherits". The result is reported as found: the Flávio-specific record on Jews/Nazism is thin and mostly inverted (he positions himself as Israel's ally and calls Lula antisemitic); the Flávio-specific record on the dictatorship, torture and Holocaust trivialisation is not thin at all, and it is in his own words.

## Method and standards

- Status vocabulary follows `README.md`: `verified` (one Tier 1, or two independent Tier 2, or one Tier 2 citing a Tier 1 document), `needs-second-source`, `dropped`.
- Tier 1 here: Senate records (senado.leg.br), the TSE-registered government plan PDF, Conib's own site, court decisions as reported with case details, Instituto Brasil-Israel's statement as quoted, original video with timestamp (C-SPAN, Poder360 YouTube upload of the Knesset speech), NBC/AP wire for the candidacy announcement.
- Tier 2 here: Folha, Estadão, O Globo, G1, UOL, CNN Brasil, BBC Brasil, Poder360, Metrópoles, Agência Brasil, Congresso em Foco, O Povo, Jovem Pan, InfoMoney, Gazeta do Povo (used only for direct quotes, flagged), Agência Pública (investigative; flagged).
- Excluded as evidence of fact: Revista Oeste, Brasil 247, Brasil de Fato, Diário do Centro do Mundo, TVT, O Cafezinho, Revista Fórum, Hora do Povo, CartaCapital opinion, Wikipedia. They are cited below only to locate a Tier 1/2 source or to state what still needs one.
- Technical limitation, stated plainly: the session's web-search budget was exhausted early, and direct fetches to folha.uol.com.br, uol.com.br, estadao.com.br, globo.com (G1/O Globo), veja.abril.com.br, reuters.com and tse.jus.br were blocked by the fetch tool. For those outlets I have the headline, outlet and date (from Google News RSS), not the body text. Where the body could not be read, the item is marked `needs-second-source` or the verbatim quote is taken from a second outlet that reproduced it. Nothing was upgraded on the strength of a headline alone.
- Archive: Wayback Machine snapshots were not queried in this session (no budget). Every URL below is "archive: pending" unless stated.
