# Axis 7: Arguments circulating in the community that the site must answer

Collected from Alice on 2026-10-05. Each entry: the argument as stated, where it comes from, what is factually true in it, and the research needed to answer it honestly. Answers are filled in after the axis reports land.

## 7.1 "Lula took Brazil out of the Holocaust remembrance alliance (IHRA)"

- Source: Instagram post by Jairo Roizen (@jroizen), 2026-10-03, reposting a Metrópoles Política card headlined "Governo Lula retira Brasil de aliança em memória do Holocausto". ~10k likes, ~1.5k comments two days after posting. URL: https://www.instagram.com/p/DeC4LQNlkOr/
- Argument as stated: in 2025 the Lula government withdrew Brazil from the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance (IHRA); for the author this alone is disqualifying. He is explicit that he is a Zionist, that Israel is not above criticism, and that Holocaust memory should not be a political instrument.
- What must be verified: date and legal form of the withdrawal (Brazil was an observer, not a member?); Itamaraty's stated justification; CONIB's and other institutions' reactions; whether the decision concerned the IHRA working definition of antisemitism and its use against criticism of Israel (Jerusalem Declaration, Kenneth Stern's own objections); which countries are members/observers; whether Brazil did anything else on Holocaust memory in 2025-2026.
- Honest framing to test: the withdrawal is real and the community read it as a signal; the site has to say so and then explain what the decision was about, without pretending it was good.

## 7.2 "Antisemitism grew in Brazil under Lula after 7 October"

- Source: common argument reported by Alice; also implicit in the comments under 7.1.
- Argument as stated: incidents rose sharply after 2023-10-07 and the government is responsible, either by rhetoric or by omission.
- What must be verified: Brazilian incident data (CONIB, FISESP, FIERJ, Safernet, Disque 100) 2022-2026; the same period in countries with strongly pro-Israel governments (Germany, UK, US, France, Argentina, Australia, Canada) from official or community monitors (RIAS, CST, ADL, SPCJ, DAIA, ECAJ, B'nai Brith); whether the Brazilian increase is proportionally smaller, similar or larger than the global pattern; what the Brazilian government did in response (Federal Police operations, prosecutions, ministry measures).
- Note from Claude: the defensible claim is "the surge was global and tracks the war, not the stance of national governments". Attributing blame to Netanyahu personally is not sourceable as a causal claim and will read as hostile to the exact audience the site wants to reach. Recommend the site does not go there.

## 7.3 "Horseshoe theory: the far left and far right converge on antisemitism"

- Source: Instagram post by @oyveygram, 2025-01-21, drawing on an essay by Dara Horn. URL: https://www.instagram.com/p/DFGbaAjSmWl/
- Argument as stated: antisemitism is cyclical and unoriginal; Soviet anti-Zionism, Nazism and the Inquisition rhyme with today's far-left "anti-Zionist" talking points; the far right wants Jews out of their countries, the far left wants Jews out of the Jewish state. Conclusion: both extremes are dangerous, so there is no reason to prefer the left.
- What must be verified: nothing to refute in the premise; left-wing antisemitism exists and the site should say so. The answer is the lethality record (axis 4): which extreme has actually killed Jews, in what numbers, and in Brazil specifically. The question is not "who is clean" but "who is more dangerous".
- Honest framing to test: concede the horseshoe as a description of rhetoric; answer it with bodies, laws and institutions. Brazil's own history (Integralismo, Vargas, Herzog) carries the argument better than Europe's.
