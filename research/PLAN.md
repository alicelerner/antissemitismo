# Research plan: "Where the danger to Brazilian Jews actually comes from"

## Context

Brazil's first-round presidential election was on 2026-10-04 and Flávio Bolsonaro finished ahead. Alice wants an interactive site aimed at undecided Jewish voters who avoid Lula because they believe he is antisemitic. The site's job is to show, with traceable evidence, that the Bolsonaro project is a far greater threat to the Jewish community than Lula.

Phase 1 (this plan) is research and material selection for the storytelling. Phase 2 (later) is turning the material into a digital experience. Nothing is built yet; the working directory is empty.

### Thesis (decided by Alice)

- Lula is a known critic of Israel, not of Jews. The site acknowledges this openly rather than arguing the "anti-Zionism is not antisemitism" line, which this audience finds worn out.
- Flávio Bolsonaro is a real threat because of: (a) his father's record and the movement he inherits, (b) Christian nationalism, (c) proximity to far-right leaders abroad with documented antisemitic histories.
- Core historical argument: right-wing extremism has been, by a wide margin, the most lethal force against Jews, in the world and in Brazil.

### Constraints already agreed

- Only absolutely reliable, traceable sources. Every item carries date, quote, source, URL, archive link, media type.
- Factual precision over rhetorical punch. The Goebbels video was Roberto Alvim (Jan 2020), fired by Jair the next day; it must be attributed correctly.
- Flávio-specific evidence is thin. The argument is built as continuity of a political project, with relationships mapped, never as direct attribution of Jair's acts to Flávio.
- Terminology follows the sources. "Far-right" or "nationalist" unless a source documents "neo-Nazi". Each foreign ally gets its own documented antisemitic episode rather than a blanket label.
- Written deliverables in English; the site itself will be in Portuguese.

## Research axes and candidate evidence

Each item below is a hypothesis to verify, not a confirmed fact. Items marked (V) are well known and only need a primary source located; items marked (?) need confirmation before use.

- (?) Growth of neo-Nazi cells in Brazil 2019-2022: Adriana Dias (Unicamp) mapping, later Safernet and Federal Police data; whether sources link the growth to the political climate.
- (?) Neo-Nazi symbols or salutes at pro-Bolsonaro events, including 8 Jan 2023. Use only with photo or video from a tier-1 outlet.
- (?) 2018 campaign episodes involving Jewish institutions (Hebraica invitation controversy, community letters).

### Axis 2: Christian nationalism

Map each with the Bolsonaro link and the ally's own documented episode.

### Axis 4: Historical lethality of the far right against Jews

- Global: the Holocaust; fascist regimes in Europe; recent mass-casualty attacks (Pittsburgh 2018, Poway 2019, Halle 2019, Christchurch as model) all by far-right perpetrators.

- Brazil: Integralismo and Gustavo Barroso's antisemitism (1930s); Vargas's secret circular 1,127 (1937) restricting Jewish immigration, documented by Maria Luiza Tucci Carneiro; Herzog's murder (1975) under the regime Jair praises; neo-Nazi cell growth (Axis 1).
- (?) CONIB and FISESP data on antisemitic incidents in Brazil by type and source.

### Axis 5: Lula's record with the Jewish community

- (V) Mar 2010: first sitting Brazilian president to visit Israel; Knesset speech; Yad Vashem.
- (V) Dec 2007: Mercosur–Israel free trade agreement signed under Lula.
- (V) Oct 2023: condemnation of the Hamas attack; "Operação Voltando em Paz" repatriation of Brazilians from Israel.
- (?) Government measures on antisemitism 2023-2026 (laws signed, working groups, Human Rights ministry actions, Holocaust remembrance events attended).
- (?) Personal relationships: Jewish figures in Lula's governments and circle; CONIB meetings.
- Honest ledger, stated not hidden: Feb 2024 Holocaust comparison and CONIB's reaction; Ahmadinejad visit 2009; ICJ position. Framed as "critic of Israel", which the thesis already concedes.

### Axis 6: Flávio-specific

- (?) Everything attributable to Flávio himself: statements on Jews, Israel, Holocaust, the dictatorship, Ustra, Alvim; who he keeps around (Malafaia, Nikolas Ferreira, Eduardo); 2026 campaign positions; any CONIB or community reaction to him.
- This axis decides how much of the site is "Flávio" versus "the project Flávio inherits". Report the gap plainly.

## Source standards

Tier 1 (sufficient alone): primary documents (official statements, Diário Oficial, court records, Yad Vashem, CONIB, government releases), original video with timestamp, peer-reviewed or university-press scholarship.
Tier 2 (sufficient when two agree or when one cites a Tier 1 document): Folha, Estadão, O Globo, G1, BBC Brasil, Reuters, AP, AFP, NYT, Haaretz, Times of Israel, Jewish Telegraphic Agency, ADL reports.
Excluded as evidence of fact: opinion columns, partisan outlets on either side, social-media screenshots without the original post, Wikipedia (use only to locate primary sources).
Each entry gets a Wayback Machine archive link at collection time.

## Execution steps

1. Create `research/` in the project with one file per axis plus `sources.md` (a table: id, date, actor, claim, verbatim quote, source, URL, archive URL, media type, tier, status).
2. Run six parallel research agents, one per axis, each with the candidate list above and the source standards. Each agent returns verified items, dropped items with the reason, and open leads.
3. Consolidate into the axis files and the sources table. Mark every item verified, needs-second-source, or dropped.
4. Write `research/00-narrative.md`: a first storytelling arc using only verified material. Preliminary arc: the fear is legitimate; where the danger historically came from; what the Bolsonaro project said and did; who its allies are; what Lula actually did; the choice.
5. Review with Alice item by item before any design work.
