## 0. Core findings (read this first)

1. **The thesis holds without qualification for the historical record.** Approximately six million Jews were murdered by the Nazi regime and its collaborators — all of them fascist, ultranationalist or authoritarian-right regimes (Vichy, Ustaša Croatia, Antonescu's Romania, Horthy's Hungary followed by the Arrow Cross, Tiso's Slovakia). No other political family in history approaches this. (Yad Vashem, USHMM — verified.)

2. **The thesis holds for the United States domestic-extremism record.** ADL's *Murder and Extremism* series attributes 100% of extremist-related killings in 2022, 2023 and 2024 to right-wing extremists (verified for 2024 via ADL press release and WaterISAC mirror). CSIS counted 57% of all US terrorist attacks and plots 1994–2020 as right-wing, nearly two-thirds in 2019 and over 90% in Jan–May 2020 (verified). Three of the four deadliest attacks on US Jews since 2018 (Pittsburgh, Poway; Halle in Germany) were by white-supremacist shooters (verified via court record reporting).

3. **The thesis does *not* hold for the worldwide tally of lethal antisemitic attacks 2015–2025, and the site must not claim it does.** Counting deaths in attacks that targeted Jews as Jews: far-right shooters killed 14 (Pittsburgh 11, Halle 2, Poway 1); jihadist/Islamist attackers killed at least 22 (Paris Hypercacher 4 and Copenhagen 1 in 2015 — unverified here; Manchester 2 and Bondi Beach 15 in 2025 — verified via Wikipedia-located BBC/Guardian/Reuters and police statements); Black Hebrew Israelite-aligned attackers killed 4 (Jersey City); "pro-Palestine"/anti-Zionist lone attackers with no organised-left affiliation killed 3 (Washington DC 2, Boulder 1); one killing had an attacker with antisemitic Nazi-themed journals judged mentally incompetent (Monsey). The December 2025 Bondi Beach massacre alone (15 killed, IS-pledged father and son) exceeds the entire far-right toll of the decade. The honest framing is: *right-wing extremism is the deadliest force against Jews across history and in the Americas; since 2015 jihadist terrorism has killed more Jews worldwide; "left-wing" attackers in the strict sense have killed almost none, but anti-Zionist-motivated individuals have killed three in the US in 2025.*

4. **German police statistics, the series most often cited for "most antisemitic crime is right-wing", carry a known methodological defect that cuts both ways.** Until end-2023 the BKA assigned antisemitic offences with no identified perpetrator to "PMK-rechts" by default (Thüringen Landtag answer Drs. 7/9560, 15 Feb 2024 — verified). The right-wing share fell from 91–95% (2015–2020) to 59% (2023) and 48% (2024) once the Middle East surge and the rule change arrived. The community monitor RIAS, which attributes only on evidence, assigns far fewer incidents to the far right (6.5% in 2024, 9% in 2025) and more to "anti-Israel activism" (26% / 23%); but RIAS also finds that most *threats* with an identifiable background come from the far right (2025 — verified).

5. **Brazil has no monitor that classifies antisemitic incidents by perpetrator milieu.** CONIB/FISESP classify by content (2025: anti-Zionism in 70.8% of validated cases, Nazi references/exaltation in 34.3%, conspiracy tropes 23.6% — verified from the PDF), and 73–81% of cases are online. The only right-wing-specific official series is the Federal Police count of *apologia ao nazismo* registrations (198 in 2020–2024, record 82 in 2023 — Metrópoles, verified). Brazil's post-7-October surge (+255% in 2023, +350% 2022→2024) is at the high end of the international band but not an outlier against France (+260%) or Australia (~+300%), and it occurred alongside comparable surges under the most pro-Israel governments (Germany, US) and the smallest surge under the most pro-Israel president (Milei's Argentina). **No correlation between government stance and surge size is visible in the data.** The site should say so.

6. **Brazil's own historical record is unambiguous in direction**: Integralismo (the only mass fascist movement in Brazilian history) produced the country's foremost antisemitic theorist, Gustavo Barroso, who published the first Portuguese translation of the *Protocols* (1936); the Vargas regime secretly barred Jewish immigration (1937) and delivered a pregnant Jewish communist, Olga Benário, to the Gestapo (1936); the military dictatorship tortured Vladimir Herzog to death and the Jewish community (Rabbi Henry Sobel) publicly exposed the lie. ### 1.1 The Holocaust and the collaborating regimes (global, qualitative)

**Death toll.** USHMM: "Six million Jewish people died in the Holocaust" — c. 2.7 million in killing centres (Auschwitz c. 1,000,000; Treblinka c. 925,000; Bełżec c. 435,000; Sobibór ≥167,000; Chełmno ≥152,000), c. 2 million in mass shootings, 800,000–1,000,000 in ghettos and camps, ≥250,000 in other violence. USHMM caveat: "no one master list of those who perished exists anywhere in the world"; estimates may move as documents surface. Yad Vashem: "approximately six million Jews"; research ranges 5.1m (Hilberg) to 5.95m (Lestschinsky); Benz 5.29m–6.0m.

**Collaborating regimes (USHMM Holocaust Encyclopedia, per-country losses).**
- **Hungary** (Horthy regency, then Arrow Cross): 564,507 Jewish dead within 1941 borders. May 15–July 9, 1944: c. 437,000 deported on 147 trains, c. 420,000 to Auschwitz-Birkenau, c. 330,000 gassed on arrival; Hungarian gendarmes, mayors and police ran the ghettoisation and deportations. After Szálasi's fascist Arrow Cross took power in October 1944, its militia shot Jews into the Danube and sent tens of thousands on death marches.
- **Romania** (Antonescu, with the Iron Guard in 1940–41): 211,214–260,000 dead in Romania proper plus 103,919–130,000 in Bessarabia/Bukovina; Iași pogrom June 1941, 13,266 killed. Secondary figures put the Antonescu dictatorship's toll at 280,000–380,000 Jews, "more than any country other than Germany" (needs-second-source: from search summary of Wikipedia/Times of Israel, not fetched).
- **Croatia** (Ustaša "Independent State of Croatia"): 30,148 Jewish dead; 12,000–20,000 murdered at Jasenovac; USHMM calls the Ustaša "fanatically nationalist, fascist, separatist, and terrorist". Croatian authorities handed c. 7,000 surviving Jews to Auschwitz 1942–43.
- **France** (Vichy): 72,900–74,000 (USHMM country table) / c. 77,000 (USHMM France article) Jews deported and killed, mostly at Auschwitz; *Statut des Juifs* Oct 1940 and June 1941; French police under Bousquet arrested 13,000 Jews in Paris on 16–17 July 1942 (Vél d'Hiv).
- **Slovakia** (Tiso): c. 60,000. **Italy** (Salò/Nazi occupation): 7,858.

#### 1.10.2 What the data show (answer the site's question honestly)
1. **The surge was global and simultaneous.** Every monitor shows a step change in October 2023; France's October–December 2023 alone exceeded all of 2022.

## 2. LIMITS OF THE DATA (write this into the site, not a footnote)

1. **"Antisemitic incident" is not one unit.** CONIB counts mostly online posts (73–81%); CST and CIDI exclude most online material; RIAS counts demonstrations with antisemitic content as incidents; ADL changed its 2024 methodology to include anti-Zionist rallies. Cross-country percentage comparisons are order-of-magnitude only.
2. **Perpetrator milieu is rarely known.** RIAS can attribute only 43% of incidents; most online offenders are anonymous; FBI, SPCJ, CIDI, DAIA, CONIB publish no milieu breakdown at all. "Most antisemitic crime is right-wing" rests mainly on German police data whose attribution rule defaulted unknowns to the right until 2024.
3. **Lethality counts are small-n and period-sensitive.** Three far-right shootings in 2018–2019 vs three Islamist/anti-Zionist attacks in 2025 can flip the ranking. Choose the window honestly and show both.
4. **Content ≠ milieu.** CONIB's "anti-Zionism 70.8%" and CST's "52% Israel-related" describe what was said, not who said it. Nazi references (34.3% in Brazil) include both neo-Nazi glorification and Israel-Nazi analogies by anti-Israel posters.
5. **Brazil has no official antisemitism statistics.** The PF *apologia ao nazismo* series captures only one crime type; Disque 100 and Safernet aggregate broader categories; CONIB's validation rules changed between 2024 and 2025.
6. **Government stance cannot be isolated.** Nine countries, one shock, different monitors: the correlation test in §1.10.2 is suggestive at best; do not claim causation in either direction.
7. **Several key figures in this dossier are flagged "memory"** (ADL 10-year share; CST 2023; B'nai Brith; ECAJ totals; DAIA 2022; 2015 Paris/Copenhagen; Halimi/Knoll; STF Ellwanger; Circular 1,127 number). They must be confirmed before publication.
