### Item 1. Clube Hebraica Rio de Janeiro, 3 April 2017 — the speech, the host, the community reaction, the legal outcome

**1a. The speech**

- **Date:** 2017-04-03 (Monday evening), Clube Hebraica, Laranjeiras, Rio de Janeiro. About 300 attendees inside, about 150 protesters outside (Jornal do Brasil count).
- **Actor:** Jair Bolsonaro, then federal deputy (PSC-RJ), self-declared presidential pre-candidate.
- **Claim:** Speaking inside a Jewish community club, Bolsonaro described a visit to a quilombo in terms of body weight measured in "arrobas" (the unit used for cattle) and said its residents were no longer fit "even as breeders"; he also said that if it depended on him "everyone will have a firearm at home", that Brazil "cannot open its doors to everyone", and praised "the Japanese" as "a race that has shame".
- **Verbatim (as transcribed in the PGR complaint and reproduced by Poder360 and Congresso em Foco):**
  - "Eu fui num quilombo. O afrodescendente mais leve lá pesava sete arrobas. Não fazem nada! Eu acho que nem para procriador ele serve mais. Mais de R$ 1 bilhão por ano é gastado com eles." / "I went to a quilombo. The lightest Afro-descendant there weighed seven arrobas. They do nothing! I think he is not even good as a breeder any more. More than R$ 1 billion a year is spent on them."
  - "Não vai ter um centímetro demarcado para reserva indígena ou para quilombola." / "Not one centimetre will be demarcated for indigenous reserves or for quilombolas."
  - "Alguém já viu algum japonês pedindo esmola? É uma raça que tem vergonha." / "Has anyone ever seen a Japanese begging? It is a race that has shame." (JB transcript)
  - "Não podemos abrir as portas para todo mundo." / "We cannot open the doors to everyone." (JB transcript, on refugees)
  - On the protesters outside: "O pessoal aí embaixo eu chamo de cérebro de ovo cozido." / "The people down there I call boiled-egg brains." (JB transcript)

  - Congresso em Foco, "Bolsonaro: 'Quilombola não serve nem para procriar'", April 2017, https://congressoemfoco.com.br/noticias/bolsonaro-quilombola-nao-serve-nem-para-procriar , archive: pending, Tier 2 (headline/lede via search; body fetch blocked).

The site should present both the words and the court's reasoning.

**1b. The host and the internal controversy in the Jewish community**

- **Date:** March-April 2017.
- **Actors:** Clube Hebraica RJ (host); Hebraica São Paulo (which cancelled a parallel invitation); Jewish youth movements and members who organised the protest; the Confederação Israelita do Brasil (CONIB).
- **Claim:** The Rio invitation went ahead after the São Paulo Hebraica cancelled a planned Bolsonaro lecture following a petition with more than 2,600 signatures from community members (JB). About 150 people protested outside Hebraica RJ, organised by "movimentos juvenis da Comunidade Judaica"; they called out the names of Jews killed under the dictatorship, Vladimir Herzog and Iara Iavelberg, answering "presente" (JB). CONIB publicly criticised Bolsonaro after the lecture (Tribuna PR/Estadão headline: "Bolsonaro é criticado por Confederação Israelita após palestra em clube judaico").
- **Verbatim CONIB note (6 April 2017, as reproduced by Tribuna PR/Estadão Conteúdo):** "A palestra de Jair Bolsonaro na Hebraica do Rio de Janeiro nesta segunda-feira, 3 de abril, provocou, como se esperava, divisão e confusão na comunidade judaica. A Conib apoia o debate político e acha que ele é sempre necessário. (...) Defendemos, porém, que esse debate tenha critérios e seja pautado, sempre, pelo equilíbrio e pela pluralidade. Nossa comunidade abriga uma grande diversidade de pensamento, e os dirigentes comunitários precisam ter isso claro para bem cumprirem seu papel. A comunidade judaica defende, de forma intransigente, os valores da democracia e da tolerância e o respeito absoluto a todas as minorias." / "Jair Bolsonaro's lecture at the Hebraica of Rio de Janeiro this Monday, 3 April, provoked, as expected, division and confusion in the Jewish community. CONIB supports political debate and thinks it is always necessary. (...) We hold, however, that this debate must have criteria and always be guided by balance and plurality. Our community houses a great diversity of thought, and community leaders need to be clear about this to fulfil their role well. The Jewish community defends, intransigently, the values of democracy and tolerance and absolute respect for all minorities."
- **Host:** the invitation came from Hebraica RJ's president, Luiz Mairovitch, after the São Paulo veto (Sul21/Estadão reporting via search; `needs-second-source` for his own justification). The JB subtitle: "Ele também afirmou que, se depender dele, 'todo mundo terá uma arma de fogo em casa'." The protest chant "Vladimir Herzog — presente!" is reported by JB.

**1c. Legal outcome**

- **Criminal track (racism, Lei 7.716/89 art. 20, and injúria):** PGR Raquel Dodge filed a complaint at the STF (Inq. 4694). On 2018-09-11 the STF First Panel rejected the complaint 3-2: Marco Aurélio (rapporteur) and Luiz Fux against receiving it; Rosa Weber and Luís Roberto Barroso for receiving it; Alexandre de Moraes cast the deciding vote against. Moraes's reasoning as reported: statements were "vulgar", "grosseiras", made in the context of "crítica política" to public policy, covered by parliamentary immunity even outside the Chamber; "o eleitor" should judge. Source: Poder360, 2018-09-11, URL above, Tier 2 citing Tier 1 (STF decision). Status: `verified`. Correction to the task brief: the dismissal was by the STF (Supremo Tribunal Federal), not the STJ.
- **Civil track (dano moral coletivo, MPF action):** On 2017-10-11 (approximate; the Congresso em Foco item carries the date) federal judge Frana Elizabeth Mendes (Justiça Federal RJ) ordered Bolsonaro to pay R$ 50,000 in collective moral damages for the quilombola remarks. Source: Congresso em Foco, "Justiça condena Bolsonaro por ofender quilombolas", https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticias/justica-condena-bolsonaro-por-ofender-quilombolas/ , Tier 2 (headline/lede via search; body blocked); Metrópoles, "Bolsonaro é condenado por declaração contra quilombolas", https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/politica-br/bolsonaro-e-condenado-por-declaracao-contra-quilombolas , Tier 2. **Appeal:** on 2018-09-25 the 8ª Turma Especializada of the TRF-2 (rapporteur Des. Marcelo Pereira da Silva), unanimously, reformed the sentence and removed the condemnation, holding that the remarks were covered by parliamentary inviolability for "opiniões, palavras e votos" (CF art. 53) because Bolsonaro "notoriamente sempre se posicionou" on spending for indigenous and quilombola lands (case 0101298-70.2017.4.02.5101). Source: Migalhas, "TRF-2 reforma sentença que condenou Bolsonaro por ofensa a quilombolas", 2018-09-25/26, https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/288094/trf-2-reforma-sentenca-que-condenou-bolsonaro-por-ofensa-a-quilombolas (fetched), legal-press Tier 2 citing the Tier 1 ruling; Migalhas on the first instance, https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/266489/bolsonaro-e-condenado-em-r-50-mil-por-ofensas-ao-povo-quilombola (26ª Vara Federal RJ; MPF had asked R$ 300,000). Status: `verified` for both instances. Net legal result of the Hebraica speech: no criminal or civil liability, in both cases on parliamentary-immunity grounds.

### Item 2. Roberto Alvim's Goebbels paraphrase (16-17 January 2020) and the Secom "o trabalho... libertará" video (11-12 May 2020)

**2a. Alvim video**

- **Date:** video published 2020-01-16 (Thursday night) on the Secretaria Especial da Cultura's official channels; dismissal 2020-01-17.
- **Actor:** Roberto Alvim, Special Secretary of Culture (Ministry of Tourism), appointed by Jair Bolsonaro.
- **Claim:** Announcing the "Prêmio Nacional das Artes", Alvim read, over Wagner's "Lohengrin" prelude and in a staged office with a cross and a Brazilian flag, a passage that reproduces almost word-for-word a Goebbels speech to theatre directors at the Kaiserhof hotel, Berlin, 8 May 1933.
- **Verbatim Alvim:** "A arte brasileira da próxima década será heroica e será nacional. Será dotada de grande capacidade de envolvimento emocional e será igualmente imperativa, posto que profundamente vinculada às aspirações urgentes do nosso povo, ou então não será nada." / "Brazilian art of the next decade will be heroic and will be national. It will be endowed with a great capacity for emotional involvement and will be equally imperative, since deeply bound to the urgent aspirations of our people, or else it will be nothing."
- **Goebbels original (English rendering used by the Brazilian press from the 1933 speech):** "German art of the next decade will be heroic, will be sternly romantic, will be objective and free from sentimentality, will be national with great pathos and equally imperative and binding, or else it will be nothing." (Metrópoles reproduces this English text; the German original "Die deutsche Kunst des nächsten Jahrzehnts wird heroisch, wird stählern-romantisch, wird sentimentalitätslos sachlich, wird national mit großem Pathos ... sein, oder sie wird nicht sein" is in the standard Goebbels speech collections; archive: pending.)
- **Goebbels original in the Portuguese rendering used by Gazeta do Povo (8 May 1933):** "A arte alemã da próxima década será heroica, será ferreamente romântica, será objetiva e livre de sentimentalismo, será nacional com grande páthos e igualmente imperativa e vinculante, ou então não será nada."

There is nothing wrong with the sentence." (CartaCapital transcript of his Facebook post; Tier 2 reproduction of a primary post.) ### Item 3. Eduardo Bolsonaro's "novo AI-5" (28-31 October 2019) and later authoritarian references

**3a. The remark**

- **Date:** interview recorded Monday 2019-10-28, published on journalist Leda Nagle's YouTube channel 2019-10-31 (Thursday).
- **Actor:** Eduardo Bolsonaro, federal deputy (PSL-SP), the president's son, then designated but never confirmed as ambassador to Washington.
- **Claim:** Asked about the risk of unrest in Brazil similar to Chile's, Eduardo said that if the left "radicalised" to the level of the late 1960s, the response could come "via um novo AI-5" (Ato Institucional n.º 5 of 13 Dec 1968, the dictatorship's decree that closed Congress and suspended habeas corpus) or via a plebiscite-approved law "as in Italy".
- **Verbatim:** "Se a esquerda radicalizar a esse ponto, a gente vai precisar ter uma resposta. E uma resposta pode ser via um novo AI-5, pode ser via uma legislação aprovada através de um plebiscito, como ocorreu na Itália. Alguma resposta vai ter que ser dada." / "If the left radicalises to that point, we will need to have a response. And a response can be via a new AI-5, it can be via legislation approved through a plebiscite, as happened in Italy. Some response will have to be given." (Exame, Estado de Minas, A Gazeta/Estadão Conteúdo concur on the wording.)
- **Reactions:**
  - Jair Bolsonaro, 2019-10-31: "Quem quer que seja que fale em AI-5 está sonhando. Está sonhando, está sonhando. (...) O AI-5 existia no passado, existia em outra Constituição. Não existe mais. Esquece." / "Whoever speaks of AI-5 is dreaming. Dreaming, dreaming. (...) AI-5 existed in the past, it existed under another Constitution. It no longer exists. Forget it." (A Gazeta/Estadão Conteúdo, fetched.)
