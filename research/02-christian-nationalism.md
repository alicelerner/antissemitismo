### A1. Slogan "Brasil acima de tudo, Deus acima de todos": military cry + Christian base
- Date / Actor: 2018 campaign and 2019–2022 government slogan; Jair Bolsonaro.
- Claim: the slogan fuses an Army paratrooper cry with a Christian clause; it was read by scholars as the symbolic seal of the military–evangelical alliance.
- Verbatim (T1, peer-reviewed): "A aliança entre as lideranças evangélicas e militares figuram justamente entre as bases primordiais de sustentação da candidatura e do governo Bolsonaro, simbolizadas no slogan de campanha 'Brasil acima de tudo, Deus acima de todos'. Enquanto o termo 'Brasil acima de tudo' representa o apoio do Exército – frase usada como brado da Brigada de Infantaria Paraquedista do Exército (Seto, 2018) – a expressão 'Deus acima de todos' expressa a base cristã."
  EN: "The alliance between evangelical and military leaderships is among the primary bases sustaining Bolsonaro's candidacy and government, symbolised in the campaign slogan 'Brazil above everything, God above everyone'. While 'Brazil above everything' represents Army support – a phrase used as the cry of the Army's Paratrooper Infantry Brigade (Seto, 2018) – 'God above everyone' expresses the Christian base."
- Sources: Lentz, Rodrigo; Penido, Ana. "Com a bíblia e com o fuzil: notas exploratórias sobre a ascensão evangélica nas forças armadas", Brasiliana: Journal for Brazilian Studies, v.10 n.2, 2021, p. 352. https://tidsskrift.dk/bras/article/download/128410/177034 — archive: pending — T1.
- Media: none needed (text).
- Status: verified.
- Nuance: the phrase itself is not antisemitic; its weight comes from what it signals (a confessional definition of the nation). The paratrooper origin ("Brasil acima de tudo" as brigade cry) is documented via Seto 2018 inside the article; Wikipedia agrees but is not evidence.

## B. Historical section (verified)
