## Status vocabulary

- `verified`: one Tier 1 source, or two independent Tier 2 sources, or one Tier 2 citing a Tier 1 document.
- `needs-second-source`: one Tier 2 source only. Not usable on the site until upgraded.
- `dropped`: failed the standard; kept in the file with the reason so nobody re-researches it.

## Source tiers

Tier 1: primary documents, official statements, court records, original video with timestamp, peer-reviewed or university-press scholarship, official statistics.
Tier 2: Folha, Estadão, O Globo, G1, UOL, BBC, Reuters, AP, AFP, NYT, Guardian, Haaretz, Times of Israel, JTA, El País, ADL reports.
Excluded as evidence of fact: opinion columns, partisan outlets on either side, social-media screenshots without the original, Wikipedia.
