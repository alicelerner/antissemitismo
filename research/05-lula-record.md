Three factual corrections to the brief, found during research:
1. **Lula did not lay a wreath at Herzl's tomb in 2010 — he refused to.** The Herzl refusal is the single most-cited Israeli grievance from that trip (Folha, BBC Brasil, O Globo, 15–16 Mar 2010). He did visit Yad Vashem and lay a wreath there. The site must not say "Herzl wreath".
2. **The federal Holocaust remembrance day is Lei 14.938/2024 (29 Jul 2024), not 14.838.** Lei 14.838/2024 is a tourism-route law (checked on Planalto). There is no federal "Dia Nacional de Combate ao Antissemitismo"; such days exist only at state/municipal level (e.g., São Paulo state).
3. **Brazil joined South Africa's ICJ case in July 2025, not July 2024.** Itamaraty note of 23 Jul 2025; Poder360 and Agência Brasil 24 Jul 2025.

### P1. March 2010 — First state visit by a sitting Brazilian president to Israel; Knesset address; Yad Vashem

- **Date:** 15–16 Mar 2010 (Jerusalem), then Ramallah 17 Mar.
- **Actor:** President Lula; hosts Shimon Peres (President) and Benjamin Netanyahu (PM).
- **Claim:** Lula addressed a special plenary session of the Knesset on 15 Mar 2010, was received by Peres and met Netanyahu, visited Yad Vashem on 16 Mar 2010, planted a tree in the Jerusalem Forest, and refused the Israeli MFA's invitation to visit Theodor Herzl's tomb; FM Avigdor Lieberman boycotted the Knesset speech and the Lula–Netanyahu meeting in protest.
- **Verbatim (Yad Vashem, 16 Mar 2010):**
  - "Aqueles que lutam pelos direitos humanos não podem de forma alguma permitir que se repita algo como o Holocausto." — "Those who fight for human rights can in no way allow something like the Holocaust to be repeated."
  - "Nunca mais, nunca mais, nunca mais." — "Never again, never again, never again."
  - "A visita ao Museu do Holocausto deveria ser quase obrigatória para todos os que querem dirigir uma nação." — "A visit to the Holocaust Museum should be almost mandatory for all who want to lead a nation."
  - "A humanidade deve repetir todos os dias, quantas vezes for necessário, 'nunca mais', 'nunca mais', 'nunca mais'." — "Humanity must repeat every day, as many times as necessary, 'never again'…"
  - Yad Vashem showed "o que pode acontecer quando a irracionalidade toma conta do ser humano" — "what can happen when irrationality takes over the human being."
  - Folha reports he told the guide that the two great tragedies of humanity were the Holocaust and slavery; the guide replied that slavery had at least ended while Jews still feel threatened.
- **Verbatim (Knesset, 15 Mar 2010, as reported):** "I dream of an independent and free Palestine living in peace in the Middle East. I believe the Palestinians and Israelis are going to share the land of their forefathers." (JTA/Jewish Journal, 17 Mar 2010). Casarões (2014, SciELO): the speech "foi respeitosamente aplaudido" ("was respectfully applauded") in Lieberman's absence.
- **Israeli reactions (verbatim):**
  - Yitzhak Eldan, chief of protocol, Israeli MFA, to BBC Brasil (16 Mar 2010): "Apreciamos o fato de o presidente ter vindo ao museu e plantado uma árvore em seu bosque. Mas nada substitui a visita ao túmulo de Theodor Herzl." … "Não diria que isso comprometeu o sucesso da visita. Houve muitos aspectos positivos, mas há ainda muitas pontes a serem construídas nessa relação."
  - Michel Jankelowitz, Jewish Agency spokesman, to BBC Brasil: the refusal was an "insulto".
  - Hagai Marom (WZO): "The decision is insulting to citizens of Israel and to Zionist communities worldwide, including the Jewish community of Brazil." (Israel National News, 15 Mar 2010)
  - Peres and Netanyahu verbatim reactions to the speech: **not retrieved** (open lead).

### P5. Anti-antisemitism policy and Holocaust memory, 2023–2026 (what is real)

**P5a. Lei 14.532, 11 Jan 2023** (sanctioned by Lula on day 11 of the term; bill originated in Congress). Makes "injúria racial" a racism crime (2–5 years), covers offences "em razão de raça, cor, etnia ou procedência nacional"; new art. 20-C instructs judges to treat as discriminatory any treatment of minorities "em razão da cor, etnia, religião ou procedência"; §2º-B punishes obstructing religious practice. [Tier 1] https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2023/lei/L14532.htm — verified. Nuance: not antisemitism-specific; applies to antisemitic injúria via "etnia/procedência/religião".

## A) IHRA WITHDRAWAL (2025) — bulletproof version

**A1. Status before 2025.** Brazil applied for IHRA observer status in September 2021 and was accepted on 9 Nov 2021 (anniversary of Kristallnacht), under the Bolsonaro government (FM Carlos França). Itamaraty then said Brazilian diplomacy would work within IHRA "to promote education and research on the Holocaust, as well as to improve national policies to combat antisemitism." Brazil never became a full member. [Tier 2] Algemeiner/JNS, 18 Nov 2021, https://www.algemeiner.com/2021/11/18/brazil-with-its-large-jewish-community-joins-ihra-as-observer-country/. [Tier 1] MRE release "Brazil joins the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance", Nov 2021 (gov.br restricted) — archive: pending. Note: observer status does not bind a country to the IHRA working definition; adoption of the definition is a separate act Brazil never took federally (several Brazilian states and cities adopted it, e.g., RS in Jan 2025 per FISESP).

**A2. The decision.** Internal decision dated **18 Jul 2025** (O Globo, citing diplomatic sources); Itamaraty informed Israel's embassy in Brasília; Israel's MFA made it public on **24 Jul 2025**, one day after Brazil announced it was joining South Africa's ICJ case (23 Jul). Legal form: withdrawal of observer status communicated diplomatically; no decree or DOU act located (IHRA observer status is not a treaty) — open lead: confirm with IHRA secretariat/MRE whether it was "withdrawal" or "non-renewal". The IHRA website (holocaustremembrance.com) blocked automated access; its observer-country list should be checked by hand.

**A3. Official justification.** There was **no public Itamaraty statement of reasons** at the time (Metrópoles 24 Jul 2025: "Itamaraty não informou os motivos"; CONIB-published column by federal judge William Douglas, 30 Jul 2025: "tomada sem explicação oficial"). Background given by diplomatic sources to O Globo and Itatiaia: the 2021 accession was "apressada"/"displicente" and had not weighed legal and financial obligations. The only on-record government rationale is **Celso Amorim** (Roda Viva, 12 Aug 2025, reported by Metrópoles): "Não podemos permitir que isso sirva para dificultar o que achamos que é a solução [para o conflito], que é o reconhecimento do Estado da Palestina" — "We cannot allow this to serve to hinder what we think is the solution, which is the recognition of the State of Palestine"; the government felt "manipulado" by the way the alliance/definition was being used. **So: officially no reasons; the one named reason on record is about the political use of the IHRA working definition, not budget.** Metrópoles, "Celso Amorim explica por que Brasil saiu de aliança do Holocausto", 12 Aug 2025, https://www.metropoles.com/mundo/celso-amorim-explica-por-que-brasil-saiu-de-alianca-do-holocausto — archive: pending.

**A4. The Metrópoles report.** "Governo Lula retira Brasil de aliança em memória do Holocausto", Metrópoles (Mundo), **24 Jul 2025, 17:33**, subhead: "Itamaraty não informou os motivos para a saída da organização, criada na década de 1990". https://www.metropoles.com/mundo/governo-lula-retira-brasil-de-alianca-em-memoria-do-holocausto — archive: pending. The same headline ran at Folha/Agência Estado on 25–26 Jul 2025 (https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/mundo/2025/07/governo-lula-retira-brasil-de-alianca-em-memoria-do-holocausto.shtml; UOL/Agência Estado 26 Jul 2025). **A7. Competing explanation / context for the definition debate (not a defence of the withdrawal).**

"The definition was not drafted, and was never intended, as a tool to target or chill speech on a college campus. In fact, at a conference in 2010 about the impact of the definition, I highlighted this misuse, and the damage it could do." https://docs.house.gov/meetings/JU/JU00/20171107/106610/HHRG-115-JU00-Wstate-SternK-20171107.pdf — archive: pending.
- Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism (25 Mar 2021): offered as an alternative to the IHRA text; core definition: "Antisemitism is discrimination, prejudice, hostility or violence against Jews as Jews (or Jewish institutions as Jewish)." https://jerusalemdeclaration.org/
- Folha TV opinion video "Lula acerta ao deixar aliança do Holocausto usada para…" (Jul/Aug 2025) — opinion; evidence only that a mainstream columnist defended the decision.
- Important: none of this was invoked by the government at the time; using it on the site as *the government's reason* would be a misattribution.
