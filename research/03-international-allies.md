# Axis 3: International far-right alliances of the Bolsonaro family, paired with each ally's own documented episode

Research date: 2026-10-05. Author: research agent (Claude). Language: English; quotes in original plus translation.

## Method and standards

- Structure is always a pair: (a) the documented Bolsonaro-family link, (b) the ally's own documented episode. Each half is sourced separately. No blanket labels: the words "antisemitic", "neo-Nazi", "Holocaust denier" appear below only inside quotation marks, attributed to the source that used them.
- Status vocabulary follows `README.md`: `verified` (one Tier 1, or two independent Tier 2, or one Tier 2 citing a Tier 1 document), `needs-second-source`, `dropped`.
- Tier 1 here: official statements (Mazsihisz, AJC, ADL, FCJE, Zentralrat der Juden, Yad Vashem, BfV), primary documents (Trump letter of 9 July 2025, Hungarian PM's official press statement, press-conference transcript), on-the-record quotes carried by Tier 2 outlets.
- Tier 2 here: Poder360, Folha, G1, Reuters, AP, NYT, Times of Israel, JTA, Jewish Insider, Jerusalem Post, JNS, Telex (Hungary), Jüdische Allgemeine, ZDF, Agência Pública, The Objective (ES), Public Sénat (FR), RTP (PT), InfoMoney.
- Excluded as evidence of fact: opinion columns (e.g. Brasil de Fato columnists), partisan outlets (Revista Oeste, Brasil 247, Outras Palavras, European Conservative, Vermelho), Wikipedia, social-media screenshots without the original post.
- Archive: Wayback Machine availability was queried on 2026-10-05; see the table at the end. Where no snapshot exists, "archive: pending".
- Limitation: the web-search budget for this session ran out after the core searches; the last round of verification was done by fetching known URLs directly. Items that could not be re-verified are marked `needs-second-source` or listed under Open leads, never silently upgraded.
