## 1. What the research did to the thesis

The brief was: Lula is a critic of Israel, not of Jews; Flávio is a real threat because of his father's record, Christian nationalism and far-right allies; historically the far right has been by far the most lethal force against Jews. Three of those four claims survived with qualifications. One did not survive as stated.

**Holds without qualification.** The historical record, worldwide and in Brazil. Six million murdered by Nazi Germany and collaborating fascist or authoritarian-right regimes (`04 §1.1`). In Brazil, the one documented case of organised, ideological antisemitism on a mass scale is Integralismo: its militia chief Gustavo Barroso published the first Portuguese Protocols in 1936, and its motto "Deus, Pátria e Família" is the one the Bolsonaros use (`04 §1.8`, `02 §A8`, `§B1`).

**Cut by Alice's review (draft 4).** Vargas, Herzog and Ustra no longer carry the Jewish argument. Vargas is read by much of the audience as the founder of trabalhismo, so the Estado Novo's 1937 circular and Olga Benário's deportation open a left/right debate the site cannot win in one screen, even though the Estado Novo was anti-communist and historians place it on the authoritarian right. Herzog was killed as an alleged communist, not as a Jew; no source in the dossier shows antisemitism as a motive. Ustra has no Jewish connection. The dictatorship material (Ustra, 1964, Flávio's 2017 book) remains true and remains Flávio's, but it is an argument about democracy, not about Jews, and it now appears only as context in movement VIb.

**Holds with a qualification that must be printed.** "The far right is the deadliest force against Jews" is true across history and in the Americas (ADL: 100% of US extremist killings 2022-2024 were right-wing; Pittsburgh, Poway, Halle were white-supremacist shooters). It is not true for the worldwide tally of lethal attacks 2015-2025, where jihadist attacks (Paris 2015, Manchester and Bondi Beach 2025) killed more. The site says "historicamente e no Brasil", never "hoje, no mundo" (`04 §0.3`, `§1.2`).

**Holds, but the ground moved.** "Lula is a critic of Israel, not of Jews" was CONIB's own position until 2024 ("distorção perversa", but not "antissemita"). In May, June and September 2025 CONIB used the word: statements "antissemitas e irresponsáveis"; "Lula mais uma vez ataca os judeus"; the UN speech echoed "uma das mais antigas acusações antissemitas da história" (`05 §C`). The site cannot say the community's institution never called him antisemitic. It can say that CONIB's president, Claudio Lottenberg, said on 28 Jan 2026 that Lula is not antisemitic (`06 §4.2`), and that the same institution sat at the Planalto table on antisemitism that same week and called the initiative "louvável" (`05 §P9`).

**Did not survive as stated.** "Flávio is a threat to Jews" in the direct sense. His own record on Jews and Israel is pro-Israel and formally philosemitic: Jerusalem antisemitism conference on 27 Jan 2026, Brazil-Israel Friendship Day law, embassy-to-Jerusalem pledge, CONIB meeting on 29 Sep 2026, a Jewish health minister who is CONIB's own president, Netanyahu's congratulations on 5 Oct (`06 §3`). Nothing he said about Jews can be read as hostile, and a court in Feb 2026 ordered a PT member to delete posts linking him to Nazi imagery (`06 §1.10`). The site loses the reader in one scroll if it pretends otherwise.

What is his, in his own words, is the architecture around the community rather than the community itself: dictatorship rehabilitation (`06 §1.1`), the 8 January arrests "nos moldes nazistas" with a Holocaust-deportation analogy, rebuked in writing by the Instituto Brasil-Israel as "banalização do Holocausto" (`06 §1.2`), "a gestapo do Lula" (`06 §1.3`), the STF as "AI-5" (`06 §1.4`), the promise to "decretar que o Brasil é do Senhor Jesus Cristo" walked back only after the bishops called it dangerous (`02 §A5`), Olavo de Carvalho as "fonte prioritária de consulta" (`06 §1.9`), and a network he claims as his ("um craque em casa", "joga no nosso time") whose members have been condemned by Jewish institutions for their own acts (`03`).

## 2. The thesis the evidence supports

Not "Lula good for Jews, Flávio dangerous for Jews". The evidence supports this:

> Two documented records. One belongs to a harsh, sometimes offensive critic of Israel's government who has nonetheless kept every institutional channel with the Jewish community open, passed the laws, and whose state has arrested the people planning to attack Jews. The other belongs to a candidate who says all the right things about Israel while rehabilitating the dictatorship and its torturers, using the Holocaust as a rhetorical weapon, promising a confessional state, and keeping company with a global movement whose members Jewish institutions have condemned by name. One of these patterns has a name in Brazilian Jewish history: it marched in green shirts under the same motto.

The question for the reader is not "who is clean". It is "which pattern has a history".

## 3. The arc (seven movements)

### I. "Você tem razão de estar com medo"
Open by granting everything the reader already feels. Print, in full and with the G1 video, Lula's 18 Feb 2024 sentence. Print CONIB's 2025 statements. Print the IHRA exit with the Metrópoles headline the reader has already seen on Instagram. No softening, no "but". The site earns the right to argue by refusing to hide (`05 §N10`, `§C`, `§A`; `07 §7.1`). Then one line: "Agora olhe o resto do registro."

### II. "O que esse governo fez, de fato"
The ledger nobody shares on Instagram. March 2010: first sitting president at the Knesset and Yad Vashem, "nunca mais, nunca mais, nunca mais" (`05 §P1`; note: he refused the Herzl tomb, never claim the wreath there). Dec 2007: Mercosur-Israel FTA. Nov 2009: Peres in Brasília. Oct 2023: "ataques terroristas", the Security Council draft condemning Hamas vetoed by the US, 13 Air Force flights bringing 1,555 people home. Nov 2023: the Federal Police break up a Hezbollah-linked plot against Jewish targets and CONIB publicly congratulates the government. Lei 14.532/2023; Lei 14.938/2024 creating the national Holocaust remembrance day; the first federal observance at Itamaraty, Apr 2026; the Jan 2026 Planalto meeting that Lottenberg called "louvável" (`05 §P4`, `§P5`, `§P9`).

### III. "A IHRA, sem atalhos"
A dedicated answer to the single most-shared argument. Observer since Nov 2021, under Bolsonaro, never a member. Exit decided 18 Jul 2025, no official reason given, which the site calls what it is: a failure. The only reason on record, Amorim on Roda Viva, concerns the political use of the working definition against Palestinian recognition, not Holocaust memory. The same government created the remembrance day the year before. CONIB, the Wiesenthal Centre and the OAS commissioner condemned the exit; CONIB campaigns for return; the site says so (`05 §A1-A9`). Kenneth Stern, the definition's lead drafter, is context for the reader, never presented as the government's reason.

### IV. "O que o projeto diz quando fala de si mesmo"
Opens in the 1930s, one screen only: green shirts, the sigma, "Deus, Pátria e Família", and Gustavo Barroso, the movement's militia chief, publishing the first Portuguese translation of the Protocols of the Elders of Zion in 1936. Tucci Carneiro calls him "o mais convicto teórico anti-semita brasileiro" (`04 §3`, `02 §B1`). Then a hard cut to 2018-2026: the same motto at Bolsonaro rallies, traced by the Jornal Nacional itself to Italian fascism and Integralismo, and reused by Flávio in his Aug 2026 video (`02 §A8`). The site does not say Bolsonaro is an integralist. It shows the slogan and lets the reader see where it came from.

Then father to son, in their own words. Jair, Campina Grande 2017: "O Estado é cristão e a minoria que for contra, que se mude." Jair, 2019: "terrivelmente evangélico". Flávio, Aug 2026: "decretar que o Brasil é do Senhor Jesus Cristo", (`02 §A2`, `§A3`, `§A5`). Then the quantitative turn: PRRI/Brookings 2023, adherents of Christian nationalism are two to three times more likely to say Jews hold too much power or are more loyal to Israel; the driver, per peer-reviewed work, is Christian in-group dominance, not hostility to Israel (`02 §C7`). Then the honest limit, on the page: no pastor in Flávio's coalition is documented saying anything antisemitic. The argument is structural. Vichy, Horthy and Franco were all "Christian national" states with small, loyal Jewish communities (`02 §B`). And Flávio attacked the Catholic bishops within a month of the Jesus decree (`02 §A6`): a community of 100,000 should not expect gentler treatment than the CNBB.

### IVb. The lens: "filossemitismo estratégico"
Placed between IV and V, short, never accusatory. The site has just conceded Flávio's pro-Israel record in full. Now it asks what that support is made of. Three sourced moves, nothing more:

1. **Who it includes and who it excludes.** Gherman: "O bolsonarismo colonizou o judaísmo"; the movement "cria seu judeu verdadeiro" and treats left-wing Jews as "não tão judeus" (`02 §C6`; Agência Pública 2023). A philosemitism that sorts Jews into real and false ones is not protection; it is a condition.
2. **What the theology says comes at the end.** Brazilian evangelical Zionism's own script, in the words of its pastors (Machado, Mariz & Carranza 2022): "O povo de Israel somente será cheio do Espírito Santo quando se converter a Jesus" (`02 §C4`). Love of Israel as the first act of a story whose last act is conversion. Ariel and Hummel for the US genealogy (`02 §C1`, `§C3`); Spector as the fair counter-voice (`02 §C2`).
3. **What it is for, politically.** Folha's own label on 27 Jan 2026: "Flávio Bolsonaro abraça filossemitismo estratégico com visita a Israel" (`06 §4.6`, body still an open lead). The Israeli flag at Bolsonaro rallies as cover (Gherman, `04 §3`). The reader is invited to notice the sequence: Jerusalem speech, Lottenberg nomination, CONIB visit, each in a campaign year, from a project whose own words about minorities are on the previous screen.

Rule: "estratégico" is Folha's word and Gherman's analysis, quoted as such. The site does not assert motives of its own.

### V. "A companhia que ele escolhe"
Pairs only, each half sourced. The Bolsonaro contact, then the ally's own episode, then who condemned it. AfD: Beatrix von Storch received by Eduardo, Bia Kicis and Jair at the Planalto in Jul 2021; CONIB and the ADL condemned the meeting itself; the AfD's Höcke called the Berlin memorial a "monument of shame" and German intelligence classified the party as extremist in 2025. Orbán: "pequeno grande irmão" in 2022, two nights in the Hungarian embassy in 2024; the anti-Soros campaign condemned by Hungary's Jewish federation. Trump, Bannon, Musk for pattern, each with ADL quotes. Milei stated up front as the counterexample. CPAC Brasil and Le Pen never used as evidence on their own (`03 pairs 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8`). Flávio's own words tie him to the network: Eduardo "um craque em casa", Nikolas "joga no nosso time" (`06 §2`).

### VI. "O governo que dizia ser amigo"
The axis 1 material, placed right after the network. Each episode with the institutional reaction, and each with the government's own answer next to it. Ordered by strength:

1. **Alvim, Jan 2020.** The Secretary of Culture reads Goebbels almost word for word over Lohengrin, on an official channel. CONIB: "inaceitável o uso de discurso nazista". Jair fires him within a day, with a note of "total e irrestrito apoio à comunidade judaica". Both on the page (`01 §2a`).
2. **Secom, May 2020.** "O trabalho, a união e a verdade libertarão o Brasil." CONIB: the link to Auschwitz is "conhecidíssima". Wajngarten, himself Jewish, calls it "ilação canalha". Note: the slogan in earlier drafts ("trabalho, disciplina e ordem") was wrong; this is the correct one (`01 §2b`).
3. **Weintraub, May 2020.** The Education Minister calls an STF search "a Noite dos Cristais brasileira" with a Warsaw Ghetto photo. CONIB: the comparison minimises "de forma inaceitável aqueles terríveis acontecimentos" (`01 §8b`).
4. **Frias, Mar 2021.** The next Culture Secretary uses Schindler's List against Covid restrictions. CONIB: "mais uma vez comparações completamente indevidas" (`01 §8c`).
5. **Martins, Mar 2021.** A presidential adviser makes the white-power sign in the Senate; convicted at first instance in Dec 2024, the court calling its meaning "incontestavelmente discriminatório". Martins, who says he is Jewish, denies it. Appeal outcome open (`01 §8a`).
6. **Von Storch, Jul 2021.** Already in movement V; CONIB "lamentou" the reception (`01 §8d`).
7. **Hitler at the gate, Nov 2021.** A supporter at the Alvorada praises Hitler's work "com as crianças"; the president answers about moral and civic education without correcting him. Show the full exchange (`01 §6c`).
8. **Flávio, Sep 2023.** The 8 January arrests "nos moldes nazistas" (`06 §1.2`). This is where the pattern reaches the candidate himself.

The point the sequence makes, without saying it: the Holocaust appears in this government's mouth again and again as a political metaphor, and each time it is the Jewish institution that has to come out and say no. Then the counterweight, also on the page: Jair's Feb 2022 note that Nazi ideology "deve ser repudiada de forma incondicional e permanente" (`01 §6f`).

What stays off the page: the São Miguel do Oeste salute (archived by prosecutors as non-intentional; use only with CONIB's words attributed and the outcome stated, or not at all), the May 2020 Alvorada "salute" (fact-checked false), anything about Nazi iconography on 8 January (none documented), the Zambelli image (targets Palestinians, not Jews; different argument).

Neo-Nazi cells (Adriana Dias's 2019-2021 series, Safernet, PF "apologia ao nazismo" counts) close this movement as data, with the sources' own words on the political climate only (`01 §5`, `04 §1.8`).

Context line, not a screen: Flávio's 2017 book calls Ustra "um homem correto, íntegro" and says 1964 was a coup only "nos livros de 'estória' do MEC" (`06 §1.1`). It belongs here as evidence of the project's relationship with authoritarianism, never as a Jewish argument.

### VII. "A ferradura tem dois lados."
Concede the horseshoe. Left-wing antisemitism exists; CONIB's content data shows anti-Zionist hostility in 70.8% of validated 2025 cases (`04 §1.8`). Then the answer, kept to what the dossier supports: worldwide and across history, the deadliest force against Jews has been the far right; in the Americas today it still is (ADL: every US extremist killing 2022-2024); in Brazil, the only mass ideological antisemitic movement was Integralismo, and its motto is back on stage (`04 §0`, `§1.5`). No claim about Brazilian bodies: the Brazilian record does not support one. The post-7-October surge was global and tracked the war, not governments: Germany and the US surged under pro-Israel governments; Milei's Argentina had the smallest surge; Brazil sat in the same band as France and Australia (`04 §1.10`). No blame assigned to any person; the table does the work. Close on the choice between two documented records, not between a saint and a villain. Working closing line, to test: "A ferradura tem dois lados. Só um deles está pedindo o seu voto com o lema dos integralistas."

## 4. Rules the site follows, printed in a "Como fizemos" page

1. Every quote is verbatim, dated, linked to the primary source and to an archive copy.
2. "Antissemita" and "neonazista" appear only inside quotation marks, attributed to the institution that said them.
3. Nothing Jair, Eduardo or an ally did is attributed to Flávio. The site says "nunca se afastou"; it never says "endossou".
4. Flávio's pro-Israel record is on the page, in his words.
5. Lula's 2024 and 2025 statements are on the page, in his words, with the G1 video.
6. The limits of the data are on the page, not in a footnote (`04 §2`).
